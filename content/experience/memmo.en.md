Memmo.me was a platform for ordering personalised video greetings from celebrities and influencers. Customers could request greetings and messages directly from the people they followed.

### Full stack team lead

I joined Memmo.me when the company had just started expanding the IT team and quickly became a team lead for a team focusing on the B2C product and conversion. As a team lead I worked closely with a product owner and was responsible for planning the work for the team, planning with stakeholders and providing estimates, and coaching teammates to help them grow. Since the company was still small, I worked with almost everything in our codebase: a React Native app, a custom-built CMS/admin tool, payments and payouts, invoicing, real-time messaging, and integrations with customer support and search services.
