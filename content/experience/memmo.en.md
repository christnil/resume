Memmo.me is a dynamic and innovative company that operates in the online personalized messaging industry. Memmo.me provides a platform where individuals can connect with their favorite celebrities and influencers to request personalized video greetings, messages, and shoutouts. The company's mission is to create memorable and meaningful experiences for users by bridging the gap between fans and their idols.

### Full stack team lead

I joined Memmo.me when the company had just started expanding the IT team and quickly became a team lead for a team focusing on the B2C product and conversion. As a team lead I worked closely with a product owner and was responsible for planning the work for the team. Planning with stakeholders and providing estimates. Coaching the team members and helping them grow. Since the company was still a small one I worked with almost everything in our code base. A react-native app, customer built CMS/Admin tool, payments and payouts, invoicing, real time messaging, integrating a customer support service and a search service and many other things.
