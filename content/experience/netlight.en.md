Netlight is a consultant company with about 1400 employees around Europe. It is a company that organically grows and has recruitment as a core function. Netlight values the individual and is organized as a network rather than being hierarchical.

During my years at Netlight I grew with the company values. We always had a lot of focus on coaching, which I still see in myself as I work with clients. Working in a team is something that is really important to me, and I always strive to help my teammates develop and enable them to fulfil their goals.
